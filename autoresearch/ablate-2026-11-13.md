# Ablations of the real binary, Lulzx/gavel (4,062 files), 2026-11-13

The stage bench (bench/plan.bend) times each stage by the line it prints, and a
line forces only what it prints. A stage's real work can land in whichever
later stage first reads its value: with an index of the replies, `gots`
went from 1.5 s to 0.01 s in the bench and `settings` from 3.4 s to 5.3 s,
and the whole plan did not move. So the bench orders stages but does not
cost them. To cost a stage, stub it out in main.bend's build (a wrong
answer is fine), time the real binary, and subtract.
